# Kanelbulle: a cookie banner, but for buns

**In one sentence:** Kanelbulle is a free, open-source spoof cookie banner that celebrates Kanelbullens dag, Sweden's Cinnamon Bun Day on October 4th.

## Short version

Instead of asking about cookies, the Kanelbulle banner presents "Our cinnamon bun policy" and invites visitors to fika. Website owners add it with a single line of code. It appears only on October 4th, with an optional countdown in the days before, and points visitors to the nearest bakery or a recipe.

## Long version

Every October 4th, Sweden celebrates Kanelbullens dag, the Day of the Cinnamon Bun. Bakeries sell cinnamon buns by the million, and offices across the country stop for fika, the Swedish coffee break.

Most websites greet their visitors with a cookie banner nobody wants to read. Kanelbulle, made by the Copenhagen design studio Blixt & Dunder, turns that familiar pop-up into a celebration. On October 4th it shows "Our cinnamon bun policy", tells visitors it is time for fika, and offers two choices: "Accept all buns" or "Only necessary buns". Links lead to the nearest bakery on a map, or to a recipe for baking your own.

Site owners can also switch on a countdown that appears in the days before, reminding visitors to get prepared. The banner speaks Swedish on Swedish websites and English everywhere else, follows the visitor's light or dark mode, and can be placed in seven positions on the page.

Kanelbulle is about 3 KB, sets no cookies and collects no data. It is free and open source.

## Background

Kanelbullens dag was started in 1999 by the Swedish Home Baking Council (Hembakningsrådet) and is celebrated every year on October 4th.
